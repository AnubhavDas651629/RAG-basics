## Overview
InsightGrid CX is an analytics product that combines customer interaction data, transaction behavior, and feedback signals to surface actionable experience insights.

## Core Features
- Unified customer journey analytics dashboard
- Churn and loyalty propensity scoring
- Real-time alerting for drop-off and conversion issues
- Experiment tracking for UX and campaign changes

## Integrations
