## Overview
IntelliContract AI is a contract intelligence platform that automates clause extraction, obligation tracking, and risk scoring across enterprise agreements.

## Core Features
- AI-powered clause classification across 140+ clause types
- Risk heatmaps with configurable policy rules
- Contract lifecycle alerts for renewals and obligations
- Multilingual OCR and semantic search

## Integrations
- SAP Ariba
- DocuSign
- ServiceNow
- Microsoft SharePoint
